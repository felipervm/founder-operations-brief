# More time for what matters

A personal, research-informed application by Felipe Mattos for the **Administrative Assistant** role listed by Jagger for a confidential, AI-native clinical-stage biotechnology company.

**Live:** https://felipervm.github.io/founder-operations-brief/

### The idea

A lean biotech team coordinating trials, manufacturing, scientific collaborators and partners needs reliable administrative follow-through. The opportunity is to protect founder time—not to build another dashboard.

A simple proposed weekly founder brief shows decisions requiring approval, work already handled, and waiting external dependencies. All entries are **fictional illustrations** inspired by the job description; none reflect the company's internal operations.

### Experience

- V3A — AI workflows and cross-functional collaboration.
- UNICEF Guinea-Bissau — mission-driven volunteering (not clinical experience).
