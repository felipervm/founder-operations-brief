What I understand about the role

This is a support role, but also an operations role.

The job description describes a small core team working across severe-disease programs, active clinical trials, manufacturing, scientific collaborators, vendors, and investors. In that kind of environment, good administrative support protects time, reduces noise, and keeps important details from getting lost.

Where I believe I would add value

Helping the founder see what needs attention — and what does not.

I do not want to overstate experience I do not yet have. What I can offer is a strong bias toward ownership, careful follow-through, comfort with AI tools, and a habit of turning scattered information into something usable.

  • Protecting attention: preparing context before meetings and keeping next steps visible.
  • Reducing friction: creating simple systems for invoices, records, and recurring admin.
  • Communicating clearly: being reliable with partners, vendors, and internal stakeholders.
A simple example

A weekly founder brief.

If I joined, one practical way I would try to add value is by making sure the founder sees the week in a concise, decision-ready format rather than through scattered updates.

Priority meetings

What is happening, why it matters, and any prep needed in advance.

Outstanding approvals

Invoices, documents, or decisions that need attention, with status and next step.

Operational follow-ups

Items involving vendors, subscriptions, travel, or logistics that should not drift.

Exceptions to flag

Anything unclear, blocked, or time-sensitive surfaced early and clearly.
